# Commercial licence — retired in 7.0.0

**You do not need a commercial licence any more. There is nothing to buy.**

From version 7.0.0, GRU953-Studio is licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Commercial use is free for everyone — including
building software with GRU953-Studio and selling it, using it inside a company
of any size, and offering it as part of a paid service.

This page is kept only so that links to it still explain what changed.

## What this replaced

Versions up to and including 6.1.0 were released under the PolyForm
Noncommercial License 1.0.0. That licence was free for personal, educational,
research and charitable use, but commercial use required a separate paid
licence arranged by email. That requirement is now removed.

## If you are on an older version

Version 6.1.0 and earlier remain under the licence they were published with —
a licence cannot be retroactively changed for a release already distributed. If
you were using an older version commercially under a paid arrangement, or were
about to ask for one, simply move to 7.0.0 and the question disappears. See
[MIGRATION.md](MIGRATION.md).

## What is still protected

The Apache License 2.0 grants **no trademark rights** (see section 6 of the
licence). The GRU953 name and the Soaring Bird logo are still governed by
[governance/TRADEMARKS.md](governance/TRADEMARKS.md) and
[governance/LOGO-USAGE.md](governance/LOGO-USAGE.md). You may build and sell
whatever you like with this tool; you may not present it as being GRU953's own
product or use the mark as your identity.
